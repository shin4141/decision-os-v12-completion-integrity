# Responsibility Boundary

Decision-OS V12 does not prescribe a universal authority model.

AI-only, human-reviewed, or multi-party workflows may be chosen depending on risk and context. The companion artifact only records the declared boundary so a future self can understand who chose the workflow and how review was handled.

Delegating evaluation to AI does not transfer responsibility to AI.

Responsibility remains with the human or organization that chooses, deploys, and relies on the workflow.
